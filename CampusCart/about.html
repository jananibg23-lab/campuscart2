Our Mission

Built by Students, for Students

CampusCart was founded with a single purpose: to give university students direct access to high-quality academic tools, study gear, and everyday dorm essentials at honest, student-friendly prices.

Why It Was Created

Ending the Struggle for Reliable Study Gear

As college students ourselves, we faced the constant frustration of hunting across multiple shops for reliable notebook paper that didn't bleed through, laptop stands that actually held up, and backpacks sturdy enough to survive heavy engineering textbooks and monsoon commutes.

Local bookstores charged high markups, while generic online marketplaces delivered unreliable knock-offs. CampusCart curates only tested, highly durable items that genuinely assist semester coursework, revision sprints, and hostel productivity.
